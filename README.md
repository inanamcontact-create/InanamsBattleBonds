
CONCEPT
=======

Inanam's Battle Bonds is a minecraft Addon for the mod of Cobblemon in the version for Neoforge. The mod adds a gimmick into pokemon battles where the trainer can jump into the battle and act, attacking, interacting and consuming items.

The trainer can only do 1 action before entering a 3 turn cooldown, the trainer interruption happens during the move selection and does not count as the move, allowing your pokemon to still move after the trainer's action.
